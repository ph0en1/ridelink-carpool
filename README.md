# RideLink Carpool App
Problem Statement: Our school suffers from large traffic jams in the morning due to amount of students entering the parking lot or getting dropped off. Carpooling is the easiest way to reduce the amount of vehicles, but its incovenient and difficult for people to reach out and find potential carpool options on their own.


The code contains algorithm returning a map with potential carpool options and their contact information, part of the ridelink application that was pitched to the school

Can process data from a cloud database ex. google sheets
